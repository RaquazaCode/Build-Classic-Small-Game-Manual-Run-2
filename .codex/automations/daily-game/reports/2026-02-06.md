## Chosen Concept
- Slug: `shrinking-snake`
- Game: Snake-style grid game
- Twist: The arena collapses inward every fixed score interval, permanently turning border rings into deadly walls. This creates escalating time pressure while preserving simple controls.

## Why This Choice
- Deterministic state transitions support clean unit tests.
- Classic controls are familiar and instantly playable.
- The shrinking-arena twist is original enough to feel fresh but low-risk to implement.
- Works well with unattended build constraints and single-session MVP scope.

## MVP Boundaries
- Included: core snake loop, score, collision, restart, pause, responsive canvas, deterministic hooks (`advanceTime` + `render_game_to_text`).
- Deferred: sound effects, difficulty presets, persistent leaderboard, touch controls.
